# Latin and Cyrillic from the first source version

A shared grid owns em, baseline, cap, x-height, overshoot zones, ascender/descender and mark heights. Shared geometry covers straight stems, round bowls, horizontal bars and mark anchors, but Cyrillic glyph recipes are independent where construction/rhythm differs. Never create Cyrillic by transliterating Latin paths. Keep script-specific spacing and kerning classes even when shapes look alike. Use Unicode scalar IDs plus stable glyph names; separate glyph repertoire from language shaping.

**V1 coverage target:** basic Latin A–Z/a–z, digits 0–9, essential punctuation/space; Russian uppercase/lowercase А–Я/а–я including Ё/ё (66 Cyrillic letters), and required acute/dieresis marks for those forms. Add `.notdef`, combining mark anchors, nonbreaking space and common quotes/dashes. This is a quality target, not a claim that the disposable spikes implement it. Phase 2+ expands Latin accents, Ukrainian/Belarusian/Serbian/Bulgarian variants (`І/і`, `Ї/ї`, `Є/є`, `Ґ/ґ`, etc.) and related localized forms after native review.

Glyph groups: Latin straight/round/diagonal/open; Cyrillic straight (`Н П Ш`), bowl (`О Ф Ю`), diagonal (`Ж К У`), asymmetric (`Д Л Я`), lowercase-specific (`б д л т ф`). Composites are useful for Ё/ё and Latin accents only when anchors, collision and interpolation are proven; flattening may be needed during build. Use top/bottom/ogonek anchors and script-aware mark positions. Define `locl` only when actual forms are authored and tested. `GSUB`/`GPOS` compile through feature source, not hard-coded in browser preview. HarfBuzz or native browser shaping verifies strings and language tags.

Quality corpus: `АВНОРСХ` homographs, `ДЛЖКУФЯ`, lowercase `бдлтф`, `ёж`, mixed Latin/Cyrillic (`AА`, `eе`), pangrams and Russian paragraphs at 10/14/24/72 px. Check counter survival, overshoot, vertical stress, spacing, mark collisions and weight extremes with native-language review. Reference: [OpenType `locl`](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/features_ko#locl), [HarfBuzz](https://github.com/harfbuzz/harfbuzz).
